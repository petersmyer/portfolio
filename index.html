The Fluency Trap

A simulation-based assessment that teaches reviewers to catch defective AI-drafted communications in a regulated lending context. The learner does the actual job — read the draft, decide whether it ships — across four cases engineered around a single reflex: the better something reads, the less carefully it gets read.

Built to be failed on the first item. Scored against a mastery rubric with no points, badges or streaks, because those mechanics measure at or near zero effect on learning. One HTML file, no dependencies, runs offline on a phone.

Every design decision in it is documented on the page with the evidence behind it — misconception-first sequencing, simulation over knowledge check, why one case in five is clean, and why restraint is scored as its own dimension. The reasoning is the work sample as much as the artifact is.
